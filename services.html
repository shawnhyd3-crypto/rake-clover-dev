  • Weekly or bi-weekly, based on how fast your lawn grows
  • Edges trimmed along beds, walks and the driveway
  • Professional equipment, sharp blades, straight lines

Spring and fall cleanup

April to May, October to November

The two big resets of the year. In spring we clear what winter left behind and get the beds ready. In fall we get the leaves up and the property tucked in before the snow.

  • Leaves, branches and debris removed
  • Garden beds cleaned and prepared
  • Perennials cut back
  • Hedges and bushes trimmed

Mulch installation

Late spring

Fresh mulch protects roots, holds moisture and keeps weeds down for the season. We prepare the beds properly first, so it lasts.

  • Weeds removed before mulching
  • Bed edges cut and defined
  • Applied at the right depth, not piled
  • Several mulch types to choose from
  • Full cleanup when we leave

Gutter cleaning

Spring and fall

Clogged gutters send water into foundations, fascia and basements. We clear them by hand, bag everything, and make sure the downspouts run.

  • Leaves and debris removed and bagged
  • Downspouts checked for flow
  • Available in spring and fall

Snow removal

December to April

A seasonal contract, so you never have to call. When 5 cm or more falls, we come and clear the driveway and walkways.

  • Driveway and walkway clearing
  • Triggered automatically by 5 cm or more of snowfall
  • Covered December through April
  • Seasonal pricing based on driveway size and service level
